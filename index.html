Where is Grihawas Aawas Yojna located?

The project is at Govind Puram Extension, NH-24, Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201017.

Which residential plans are available?

The available residential layouts are T1 at 775 sq. ft. MSSA and T2 at 822 sq. ft. MSSA, subject to current availability.

What is the application money?

The application money is ₹25,000 for T1 and T2 residential plans. It is refundable for non-allottees, subject to applicable terms.

Are commercial shops available?

Commercial Plaza shop plans T1, T2 and T3 are listed. Contact the project team for current availability and terms.

Can I view current construction progress?

Yes. The gallery includes uploaded on-site photographs of multiple towers and the full project site.
