Refund principle

100% Payment Refundable if Not Allotted.

Applicable terms

The official application, allotment and project terms applicable to an applicant will govern the registration payment and any refund. If there is any difference between this summary and those official terms, the applicable official terms will prevail.

Applicants should review the current application and allotment documents before making a payment and retain their payment and application records.
