Our Story

A community designed for daily life, not just move-in day

Grihawas Aawas Yojna is a residential and commercial affordable-housing development at Govind Puram Extension, NH-24, Ghaziabad. Its uploaded master plan brings residential blocks, a commercial plaza, internal circulation and community amenities together across a planned site.

Actual construction photographs and the supplied plans are presented throughout this website so visitors can review the project before speaking with the team.

Our Vision

To create residential communities that improve how people live, not just where they live.

Our Mission

Present available plans, project information and current construction progress clearly.

Our Values

Quality construction, honest marketing, and long-term accountability to residents.
